# Celestial Sage (天仙) — design

An original sage-god for Fighting Styles: a mortal who takes up the heavens. Palette: void black, eclipse gold
(#F2C14E), corona white. Nothing here is taken from the other styles (their moves, animations, renderers or sounds).

## Form
- **Mortal**: staff-less, the basic rite only.
- **G — Heaven's Mantle** (cutscene, 6 s): the sky dims, nine black *Null Orbs* condense out of the air into a slow
  wheel behind the back, a thin gold halo closes over the head, the eyes take the gold, a pulse throws everything
  back. Ascended: flight, speed, strength, resistance; the orbs and halo follow him; the *Nullstaff* (black, ringed
  in gold) forms in the hand. **G again** lets it go.

## Techniques (ascended)
| Key | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| LMB | Staff Rite | thrust, sweep, overhead: gold crescent lines, black ripples |
| R | Null Orbs | the nine orbs fire one after another, home in, and erase whatever they touch |
| E | Heaven's Pull | the target is dragged in along a gold thread and burst away point-blank |
| T | Mirror Step | he vanishes; his echo strikes from behind the target, then he is there |
| Y | Starfall | (cutscene) a burning star is called down out of the sky; a second follows |
| Z | Thornwood | gold-barked, black-thorned trees erupt in a cone and throw everything skyward |
| X | Colossus | (cutscene) a giant of gold light rises behind him and cleaves the land |
| H | Eternal Eclipse | (cutscene) a black sun covers the sky, then a ring of light seals everything in reach |

## Feel
Calm, unhurried, absolute: slow camera, long holds, then sudden, enormous impacts. Sound is bells, choir-like
drones and gravity, not fire and steel.
