# Santoryu: Way of the Demon Swordsman

Forge 1.20.1 mod (needs Player Animator). Three swords, nine swords, the King of Hell: ten sword techniques with
full-length cinematics, tick-held anime impact frames, slash trails, haki grading and body animation.

Controls: **R** use the selected technique, **Z / X** previous / next, hold **Sneak** to skip a cinematic.
Carry Wado Ichimonji, Sandai Kitetsu and Enma (crafting or creative tab "Santoryu") and hold one of them.

Build: `./gradlew build` → `build/libs/santoryu-1.0.0.jar`.
