# Silver Instinct

An original martial-arts awakening mod for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1 (needs Player Animator). You fight as yourself, in
your own skin, and climb through two awakened forms. Each form has its own cinematic transformation, anime impact
frames, a cel-shaded light aura (no vanilla particles), and heavy synthesized sound.

## Forms
- **Base**: no techniques. Press **G** to awaken.
- **Instinct Omen** (G): a flickering silver-blue aura. The body slips some attacks on its own.
- **Silver Zenith** (G again, from Omen): a blazing silver aura, light trails and lightning. It slips most attacks and
  counters anyone who swung from close by.
- **Sneak + G** lets the form go.

## Keys (all rebindable)
| Key | Technique | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| LMB (empty hand) | Strike combo: jab, jab, roundhouse | Omen |
| R | Crescent Kick | Omen |
| E | Starfall Volley | Omen |
| T | Afterimage Step | Omen |
| Y | Gathered Wave | Omen |
| Z | Silver Serpent Flurry | Omen |
| X | Meteor Combination | Omen |
| C | Instinct Rush | Zenith |
| V | Gathered Sphere | Omen |
| H | Final Radiance | Zenith |

While awakened, vanilla keys that share these keys (inventory, chat, drop...) are held back.

## Config
- Client: cutscenes, title cards, impact frames, flash, shake, bloom, aberration, grain, aura detail.
- Server: damage, cooldowns, cinematic invulnerability, dodge chances, counters.
