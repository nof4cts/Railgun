# Worldbreaker

An original Forge 1.20.1 mod (needs Player Animator): **The Endless March**. Press **J** (rebindable) and a line of
colossal stone giants with molten seams claws its way out of the earth ahead of you and walks through the world,
straight over you, flattening everything in its path.

- A 15-second cinematic for the caller: tremors, the dust wall on the horizon, burning eyes through the haze, a foot
  slamming down, a giant stepping over the lens, the whole line from the air, and a crane up as a foot lands beside you.
  Anime impact frames on the big footfalls, title card, subtitle lines. Hold sneak to skip.
- 25 giants, ~50 blocks tall, in two staggered ranks. Two-segment legs and arms, weighted gait, heads that look around,
  hanging jaws; 1024px basalt texture with glowing molten seams and burning eyes.
- Real destruction: each giant ploughs a slab through everything in front of it, every footfall punches a crater and
  throws real blocks into the air; anything caught underfoot is crushed. The caller is never hurt (configurable).
- Steam pouring off hot stone, dust rings rolling out from every step, embers, molten cracks racing across the ground,
  a rust-red sky and haze, and camera shake that grows as they get closer. Every sound synthesized.
- `/worldbreaker march [yaw]` for operators.

Config: giant count, duration, cooldown, ops-only, block destruction on/off, blocks per tick, debris, damage, caller
safety; client: cutscenes, impact frames, flash, shake, bloom, smoke budget, atmosphere.

Built by GitHub Actions on this branch; the jar and in-game screenshots land on `worldbreaker-build`.
